About Us

Designed to be kept.

Fashion Hub was founded on a simple belief: beautiful clothes should be made well, priced fairly and worn for years.

Our Story

It started with one perfect jacket.

Our founder spent years searching for a jacket that was beautifully cut, made from honest materials and didn't cost a month's rent. When she couldn't find it, she designed it herself.

That jacket sold out in a week. Today, Fashion Hub designs complete collections for women and men — but the idea hasn't changed. Every piece has to earn its place in your wardrobe.

What We Stand For

Our Values

01

Craft over trend

We design for longevity, not for the moment. Classic silhouettes, refined each season, made by skilled hands.

02

Honest materials

Organic cotton, responsibly sourced wool, recycled cashmere. We tell you exactly what each piece is made of.

03

Fair for everyone

Fair prices for you, fair wages for the people who make our clothes. We visit every partner atelier ourselves.

Our Process

From sketch to your wardrobe.

Each collection begins with months of research, fabric sourcing and hand sketching in our New York studio. Every design goes through at least three rounds of sampling and is fitted on real people before it's approved.

We produce in small batches to avoid waste, and we'd rather sell out than overproduce.
